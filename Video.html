Video tag ("<video>"....."</video>") - [Both Opening & Closing tags required]

Description:- The <video> tag is used to embed video content into an HTML document, such as movie clips, tutorials, or promotional reels. Introduced in HTML5, it eliminated the historical need for proprietary Flash plugins.

Autoplay Policies & Accessibility Tracks:

• Autoplay Rule: Modern browsers block videos with autoplay unless the muted attribute is also present (e.g. <video autoplay muted loop>).
• Subtitles & Captions (<track>): Always use nested <track kind="subtitles"> or <track kind="captions"> for deaf and hard-of-hearing accessibility!
• Multiple Formats: Use nested <source> elements (MP4, WebM) for cross-browser playback.

Example:- Video player with poster thumbnail and controls:

Key Attributes:-

  • controls :- Displays built-in browser play/pause/volume controls.
  • poster :- URL of an image thumbnail shown while the video downloads or before playback begins.
  • autoplay & muted :- Automatically plays video (must be muted).
  • loop :- Restarts video automatically upon reaching the end.
  • preload :- Hints how much to pre-buffer (none, metadata, auto).

Use cases:-

  • Hero section background video banners (using autoplay muted loop).
  • Product demonstration videos and e-learning lectures.
