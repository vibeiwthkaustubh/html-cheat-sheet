Del tag ("<del>"....."</del>") - [Both Opening & Closing tags required]

Description:- The <del> (Deleted Text) tag is used to represent a range of text that has been removed or deleted from a document. Browsers render it with a strikethrough (line through the text) by default. It is frequently paired with the <ins> (Inserted Text) tag to track editorial revisions.

Standards & Discontinued Alternatives Note:

In HTML4, <strike> was used to strike out text. In HTML5, <strike> is obsolete and discontinued. The <s> tag is now used for text that is no longer accurate/relevant (like an old price), while <del> has full semantic meaning for document edits and revisions, supporting citation and timestamp attributes.

Example:- Tracking product price discount and text changes:

Special Festival Offer: $99.99 $49.99 (50% Off!)

Meeting schedule: We will meet on Thursday Sunday.

Attributes:-

  • cite :- URL pointing to a document explaining why the text was deleted.
  • datetime :- Specifies the machine-readable date/time when the text was removed (YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ss).

Use cases:-

  • Displaying original vs discounted sale prices on e-commerce sites.
  • Documenting legal contracts, terms of service, and Wikipedia editorial revision histories.
  • To-do lists showing completed/struck-through tasks.
