Mark tag ("<mark>"....."</mark>") - [Both Opening & Closing tags required]

Description:- The <mark> tag represents text that is marked or highlighted for reference or relevance purposes due to its relevance in another context (most commonly highlighting search results or callout phrases). Browsers render it with a bright yellow highlighter background by default.

<mark> vs <strong> vs <em>:

• <strong>: Indicates that content has strong importance or urgency.
• <em>: Adds stress emphasis changing vocal pitch.
• <mark>: Highlights text strictly for user relevance, like matching keywords in a search engine query.

Attributes:-

  • Global Attributes :- class, id, style.

  • Highlighting user search query terms in search engine results.
  • Highlighting passages of quoted text in academic book excerpts.
