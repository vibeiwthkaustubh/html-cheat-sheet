Footer tag ("<footer>"....."</footer>") - [Both Opening & Closing tags required]

Description:- The <footer> tag defines a footer for a document or an individual section. A footer typically contains authorship information, copyright data, contact links, sitemaps, back-to-top links, and related legal documents.

Semantic Flexibility Note:

A page is not limited to only one <footer>! While there is typically a global footer at the bottom of the <body>, individual <article> and <section> elements can also contain their own internal <footer> tags (for instance, showing tags, author metadata, or comment counts for a blog post).

Example:- A styled site footer:

Kaustubh's Web Development Portal

Privacy Policy | Terms of Service | Contact Us

© 2026 All Rights Reserved.

Attributes:-

  • Global Attributes :- class, id, style, etc.

Use cases:-

  • Global site footer with copyright, links, and contact addresses.
  • Article-level footers with publication date, author bio, and sharing buttons.
