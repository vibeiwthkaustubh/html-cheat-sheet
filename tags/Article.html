Article tag (<article>.....</article>) - [Both Opening & Closing tags required]

Description:- tag is used for independent, reusable content blocks like blog posts, news stories, or comments. It improves semantic structure, accessibility, and makes content easier to share or syndicate.

Example:- Block present below represents a independent section written inside the article tag.

This is the heading of the article

September 13, 2026

This is the paragraph of the section you can take it as a blog post or a news story with the h1 headline or a normal post by a user.

Use cases:-

  • Blog Posts
  • News Articles
  • Forum Posts / Comments
  • Product Listings
