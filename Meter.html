Meter tag ("<meter>"....."</meter>") - [Both Opening & Closing tags required]

Description:- The <meter> tag represents either a scalar measurement within a known range, or a fractional value. Examples include disk usage, battery level, or temperature ratings.

<meter> vs <progress> Distinction:

• Use <progress> to show the task completion progress of an activity (like a file download or multi-step wizard).
• Use <meter> for a static measurement within a fixed scale (like disk space, exam grades, or battery charge).

Example:- Disk space usage gauge and test score meters:

Disk Usage (75 GB of 100 GB):
75%

Student Exam Score (92 out of 100):
92%

Key Attributes:-

  • value (Required) :- The current numerical value.
  • min & max :- Lower and upper bounds of the range (defaults to 0 and 1).
  • low & high :- Range boundaries indicating "low" and "high" warning states.
  • optimum :- Indicates the ideal optimal value point in the range.

Use cases:-

  • Storage meters (RAM usage, Cloud storage capacity).
  • Battery percentage indicators.
  • Survey satisfaction ratings.
